PavingOS Product Requirements

PRD: PavingOS — Vertical Operating System for Paving Contractors

1. Summary

PavingOS is a vertical operating system for SMB paving contractors ($2M–$20M revenue) focused on fixing the most painful, expensive blind spot in the business:

Estimates are wrong, and no one knows why until it’s too late.

The product’s wedge is an estimating → job costing feedback loop that helps owners price work correctly, understand margins, and professionalize operations without adding office overhead.

This is not generic construction software.

This is paving-specific execution software, designed for commercial and light municipal work.

2. Target Users & ICP

Company Profile (ICP Sweet Spot)

Revenue: $2M–$20M

Stage: Owner-operator transitioning to professional management

Work Type: Commercial + light municipal

Trade Focus: Asphalt paving (initially)

Tech Stack Today: Excel + QuickBooks + duct tape

Explicitly out of scope (for now):

<$2M mom-and-pop shops (too price sensitive, chaotic)

$20M+ mid-market / enterprise (too much reporting + integration drag)

Residential paving (homeowner chaos, thin margins)

Primary Users

Owner / GM

Wants confidence in pricing

Wants margin visibility before mistakes repeat

Still close to day-to-day ops

Estimator (often the owner)

Needs fast, defensible estimates

Wants templates grounded in reality, not guesswork

Ops / Office Manager

Needs clean handoff from estimate → job → invoice

Wants fewer manual corrections

Foreman (Secondary)

Needs minimal-friction job execution

Not interested in “software,” only speed

3. Core Jobs-to-Be-Done

Owner / Estimator

Price jobs accurately and consistently

Understand why margins miss

Improve estimates over time using real data

Ops

Turn approved estimates into executable jobs

Capture job outcomes with minimal friction

Invoice faster, with fewer errors

Foreman

Know what the job is, where to be, and what’s expected

Record what actually happened without paperwork

4. Problems & Pain Points

Estimating

Estimates live in Excel and intuition

No feedback loop from actual job performance

Pricing errors repeat silently

Job Execution

Field data is incomplete or delayed

Paper tickets, texts, memory-based reporting

Actuals captured too late to matter

Financials

Invoicing lags job completion

Change orders missed

Job profitability discovered after the fact

Estimator

As an estimator, I want real job data baked into future estimates.

Ops

As ops, I want jobs to flow cleanly from estimate to invoice.

Foreman
